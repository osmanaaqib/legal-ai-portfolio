        "# Legal AI Twin Simulator\n",
        "\n",
        "#A demonstration of how institutional context — a firm's risk posture and\n",
        "#negotiation positions — can be encoded into a legal AI system.\n",
        "\n",
        "## The Problem\n",
        "\n",
        "#Generic legal AI produces generic output. It does not know that Firm A\n",
        "#requires mutual indemnification and Firm B accepts one-way indemnification\n",
        "#favoring its client. It does not know that Firm A caps liability at 1x fees\n",
        "#and Firm B accepts 3x with carve-outs. When two lawyers at different firms\n",
        "#ask the same question, they get the same answer, which means the system\n",
        "#is not usable as a drafting tool for either of them.\n",
        "\n",
        "#HAQQ's \"legal AI twin\" concept addresses this directly: each firm gets a\n",
        "#system that learns and reflects its specific positions.\n",
        "\n",
        "## What This Project Does\n",
        "\n",
        "#Simulates two synthetic law firms with opposing risk postures (conservative\n",
        "#and commercial). Each firm has a structured \"playbook\" of positions on four\n",
        "#standard contract clause types. The same contract clause is submitted to\n",
        "#both twins. Each twin retrieves its own playbook positions and generates a\n",
        "#redline recommendation.\n",
        "\n",
        "#The evaluation measures:\n",
        "\n",
        "#- **Adherence** — does each twin's recommendation reflect its own playbook?\n",
        "#- **Divergence** — do the two twins actually produce different output on the\n",
        " # same clause?\n",
        "\n",
        "#If both are high, the twin concept works. If adherence is high but divergence\n",
        "#is low, the twins are not differentiated. If divergence is high but adherence\n",
        "#is low, the twins are hallucinating positions they do not hold.\n",
        "\n",
        "## Why This Matters for HAQQ\n",
        "\n",
        "#Every HAQQ deployment is a \"legal AI twin\" — a system tailored to one firm's\n",
        "#or one institution's practice. The mechanism that makes that possible is the\n",
        "#playbook: a structured record of positions that the model retrieves and\n",
        "#applies. This project demonstrates the mechanism at small scale and shows\n",

        "\n",
        "def extract_json(text):\n",
        "    \"\"\"Depth-tracking JSON extractor. Same as Projects 1 and 2. The model\n",
        "    sometimes leaks reasoning preamble even in JSON mode.\"\"\"\n",
        "    if not text:\n",
        "        return None\n",
        "    text = re.sub(r'```(?:json)?\\s*', '', text).replace('```', '')\n",
        "    start = text.find('{')\n",
        "    if start == -1:\n",
        "        return None\n",
        "    depth = 0\n",
        "    for i in range(start, len(text)):\n",
        "        if text[i] == '{':\n",
        "            depth += 1\n",
        "        elif text[i] == '}':\n",
        "            depth -= 1\n",
